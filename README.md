# NourishNet

> Turn surplus food into community meals.

NourishNet is an open platform that connects surplus-food donors — restaurants, farms, supermarkets, and individuals — with verified community organisations and volunteers so that usable food is redistributed rather than discarded. Every kilogram of food redirected is tracked, measured, and reported as real social impact.
