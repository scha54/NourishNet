# Requirements Document

**NourishNet Impact**

## Introduction

**Spec 5 — Impact Dashboard**

This spec covers the public and authenticated impact dashboard: metrics calculation, aggregation, display, and the documented methodology that makes NourishNet's social impact claims credible to funders and partners.

This spec depends on Spec 1 (Foundation) through Spec 4 (Pickup) being complete, because impact data is generated by completed redistribution events.
